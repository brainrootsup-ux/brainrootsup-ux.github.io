Privacy Policy – Clips Virales

Last updated: 7 October 2026

What the app does

Clips Virales is a desktop tool used by a single person (its author) to create short clips from their own videos and publish them to their own TikTok and YouTube accounts using the official APIs.

Data we access

  • Basic TikTok profile information of the connected account (user.info.basic), to confirm which account is connected.
  • Permission to publish videos to the connected account (video.publish).
  • The list and statistics of the account's own public videos (video.list), to see how the published clips perform.

How data is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the author's own computer, encrypted with the operating system's protection (Windows DPAPI). No data is sent to any server other than the official TikTok and YouTube APIs.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared or transferred to third parties.

Deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions). Revoking it, or deleting the local token file, removes all access.
