Clips Virales

Clips Virales is a personal tool that turns long videos owned or licensed by its author into short vertical clips with subtitles, and publishes them on the author's own social media accounts (TikTok, YouTube Shorts) through their official APIs.

The tool is used only by its author to manage their own accounts. It does not collect data from other users.
